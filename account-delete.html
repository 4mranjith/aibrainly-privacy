Delete your account: AiBrainly: AI Journal

Last updated: 5 October 2026. You can delete your AiBrainly: AI Journal account and its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open AiBrainly: AI Journal and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email ranjithstar256@gmail.com from the email address or phone number linked to your account, with the subject Delete my AiBrainly: AI Journal account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in identity, and journal entries, memory profile and AI chat history, plus usage data tied to your account.

What may be kept

Anonymised crash and diagnostic data that is not tied to you, and purchase records that Google Play or tax law requires us to retain.
